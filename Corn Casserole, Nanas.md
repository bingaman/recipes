# Corn Casserole, Nanas
> **tags**: #Quick Bread #5star

## Description


## Ingredients

- • 1/4 pound butter, melted
- • 1 (15 ounce) can whole kernel corn, drained
- • 1 (15 ounce) can creamed corn
- • 1 (8.5 ounce) package corn bread mix.  I use Jiffy Mix
- • 1 ounce sour cream

## Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).

2. Combine butter, whole corn, creamed corn, cornbread mix, and sour cream in a mixing bowl.

3. Fold the ingredients together, and pour into a 2-quart casserole dish.

4. Bake in the preheated oven for 1 hour.
